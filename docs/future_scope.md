# Future scope (explicitly NOT current objectives)

These ideas are related and may become future work. They are parked here so they do not expand the current study. Adding any of them requires showing it is necessary to answer the minimal question (discipline rules 6 and 9).

- **Full Diagnosis-as-Interface architecture:** separate internal representation, decision optimization, and human/institutional label rendering.
- **Adaptive examination planning:** sequential test selection policies.
- **Value-of-information:** optimizing measurement acquisition by expected information gain.
- **Closed-loop clinical control:** adaptive sensing and treatment adjustment over time.
- **Economic measurement optimization:** cost-aware acquisition and reimbursement modeling.
- **Governance and interface design:** auditability, accountability, clinician-facing presentation, coding and billing.

Also out of scope for now: causal treatment-effect optimization, outcome-improvement claims for foundation models, and reconstruction of clinicians' private reasoning.
